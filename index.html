Lead generation and contact enrichment

Finding group-business prospects around a hotel meant searching Google one business at a time. Now you pick a property, a radius and an industry in an Appsmith app, and it pulls matching local businesses through the Google Maps API. The real work happens in an n8n backend I built: a form kicks off a contact-scraping workflow that crawls each business website with Hyperbrowser, parses emails, phone numbers and addresses with JavaScript, writes them to a tracking sheet, and posts on Slack when the run finishes or a site can't be reached. I worked directly with the CTO to get the API and scraping access it needed.

  • Property, radius, industry
  • Google Maps search
  • n8n + Hyperbrowser crawl
  • Tracking sheet
  • Sales and Opsteams prospecting with it
  • Since Feb 2026running in production

n8n, Hyperbrowser, JavaScript, Appsmith, Google Maps API, Google Sheets, Slack

SARAH: a team product I helped shape

SARAH is an n8n product built by Atica's Tech team together with Revenue, Sales and Operations. I joined the core team in November 2025 as the Operations member.

Automated RFP submission on HotelPlanner

HotelPlanner RFPs used to be found and submitted by hand, and average turnaround ran past 24 hours. SARAH signs in to the HotelPlanner portal for each property, finds new RFPs with Hyperbrowser, gets the rate from the pricing model or alerts a Revenue associate, submits a proposal that matches the guest's request, and emails the stakeholders.

  • New RFP found
  • Rate from model or Revenue
  • Proposal submitted
  • Stakeholders emailed

n8n, Hyperbrowser, PostgreSQL, Metabase, Excel

  • 200+properties live
